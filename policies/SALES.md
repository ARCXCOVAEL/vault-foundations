# Sales and founding offer

ARCXCOVAEL LLC - policy revision vf-1.0-20261026.

## One-time purchase

Vault Foundations offers one named person permanent 1.x use, including all 1.x
updates released, across their authorized devices and vaults. Later major
upgrades are optional. There is no automatic subscription or renewal charge.
The product does not promise indefinite support, hosting or future compatibility.

The one-time price is US$5.79 for eligible founders and US$8.95 otherwise, before
applicable tax. These are total product prices before tax, not a base price plus
an undisclosed fee. The checkout displays the seller, currency, tax and final
total before payment. Eligibility and the final quoted total govern the order.

## Founders window

The founders window lasts the first three UTC calendar months from actual public
production launch. The announced launch and cutoff timestamps will be displayed
before enrollment opens. Everyone who
starts the original 30-day production trial before that cutoff retains founders
pricing until the later of the general cutoff and that trial's original end.
Download or sign-in alone does not start a trial or reserve founders pricing.
Reinstall, a new vault or device, reconnect and update do not renew eligibility.

An already-open, server-created checkout retains its quoted price if payment
completes after its price deadline. Newly opened checkouts use current eligibility.
A trial creates no automatic purchase, subscription or stored-card obligation.

## Trial and safe fallback

The trial begins with explicit first production use after account sign-in and
terms acceptance. It lasts 30 days. Online activation and periodic checks are
required; a signed offline allowance lasts up to 24 hours after a successful
check and never past the original trial end. Purchased 1.x proofs have no
recurring renewal expiry. Account recovery after losing a proof requires
connectivity and reasonable verification.

Expiry pauses new paid navigation upkeep. Your original notes, existing generated
pages, annotations, ordinary links, editing, early filtering, performance controls
and recovery remain available. No remote wipe is authorized.

## Version timing, continuing support and optional upgrades

There is no fixed interval between major releases and no guaranteed minimum
time before the next major version. A new major version may arrive sooner or
later than expected. We continue to support 1.x under this Support policy when
a later major version is released; introducing 2.0 does not itself end 1.x
support or shorten an existing permanent 1.x license.

Your purchase includes the 1.x updates that we release. Future major versions
are optional separate upgrades and may have different prices, features and
terms, disclosed before purchase. You are not required to buy a new major
version to retain your existing 1.x rights. Any later change in support scope
will be communicated through updated product documentation, while preserving
accepted license rights and applicable requirements. This is not a promise to
release updates on a fixed schedule or to support every future host version.
