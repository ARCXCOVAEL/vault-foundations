By **ARCXCOVAEL LLC**. Local-first desktop software for early vault filtering,

## What it does

- Filters excluded files early and supplies bounded performance controls, status and recovery.
- Maintains folder-index, root and optional Home navigation that links to original documents.
- Preserves ordinary creation, editing, moves, renames, note contents and links.
- Keeps necessary licensing and purchases separate from local vault processing.

## Trial and purchase

One explicit 30-day production trial follows account sign-in and acceptance.
No automatic charge or subscription is created. Trial checks allow up to 24
hours offline after a successful check, never beyond the original trial end.

Prepared one-time prices are **US$5.79 founders** and **US$8.95 regular**, before
applicable tax. The founders window is the first three calendar months from
launch. Eligible trials retain that price for their full original 30 days;
already-open server-created quotes are honored.

A named-person purchase covers permanent 1.x use and all released 1.x updates
across their authorized devices and vaults. Purchased proofs work offline
without recurring license renewal. Future major upgrades are optional and may
have different prices and terms. Major-release intervals vary. A later major
release does not itself end 1.x support or withdraw accepted 1.x rights.

If full navigation access expires, new paid automation pauses while notes,
ordinary editing, links, existing generated pages and free protections remain.
All sales are final except remedies required by law or the payment provider.
